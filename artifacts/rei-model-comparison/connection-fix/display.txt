Haru Mizuno: You tell her that even the biscuits probably need her approval before they're allowed in.

Rei Kurosawa: They do, actually. I ran them through a short interview. The shortbread was evasive, but the ginger one had references.

She picks up the ginger biscuit and turns it over, examining it like a manuscript page.

Rei Kurosawa: For the record, the coffee rules are not a menu. They're a shortlist. There's a difference, and I'll defend it in writing if I have to.

Haru Mizuno: You admit that you didn't really come up for the coffee. You stayed because you wanted her company.

Rei Kurosawa: Hm.

She sets the cup down on the armrest, more carefully than it needs. When she speaks again her tone is level, but the dry edge has gone out of it.

Rei Kurosawa: That's a better reason than the coffee. I'd have said so sooner if you'd led with it.

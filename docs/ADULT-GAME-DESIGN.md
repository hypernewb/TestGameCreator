# Adult-game design principles

The project is building an adult eroge/dating sim, not merely a generic narrative engine that happens to permit explicit prose. Adult mechanics should create playable decisions before, during, and after intimacy.

- Separate affection, attraction, satisfaction, sexual openness, consent, and reputation. One high number must not substitute for every relationship dimension.
- Mix route shapes: slow-burn scene-gated romance, ordinary dating, automatic short routes, recurring casual partners, one-off encounters, unavailable characters, and non-monogamous agreements.
- Make time and opportunity matter. Work shifts, private-room costs, schedules, phone invitations, and missable events create tradeoffs similar to time-slot social games and real-time messenger dating games.
- Make privacy consequential. Witnesses, gossip, disclosure, jealousy, partner agreements, and reputation should respond to what actually happened and who could know.
- Treat aftermath as gameplay. Aftercare, satisfaction, cooldowns, memories, pregnancy/health systems if later authored, and relationship renegotiation are more meaningful than using explicitness itself as a reward tier.
- Preserve agency. Every participant is explicitly adult; consent is specific, current, and withdrawable. Easy difficulty and promiscuous characterization reduce courtship requirements but never remove consent or hard boundaries.
- Treat reader convenience as part of consent UX. Auto-forward and skip-read stop at choices and intimacy boundaries; the searchable backlog makes prior wording easy to review; reduced motion, scalable text and high contrast remain available during explicit scenes.

The three cross-setting templates intentionally include adult modules in different settings. Their content is small; their job is to prove that erotic mechanics are modular rather than campus-specific.

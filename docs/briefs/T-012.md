# T-012: Relationship stages + repairable breakups (build, thin slice)
Role: implementer · Platform: codex · Milestone: O2

## Goal
Each heroine moves acquaintance → dating → exclusive. If Trust stays low she shows strain, then breaks up; the breakup scars stats once and is always remembered, but she can be won back and the relationship restarts at dating.

## Do
1. Pack `relationshipStageSettings`: stages `acquaintance|dating|exclusive`, `strainTrustFloor` (default 20), `strainDays` 2, `scar {trust:-10, chemistry:-10}`, `repairDelayDays` 2, `repairTrustBar` (default = the existing dating-entry Trust threshold). Missing field = legacy behaviour (non-campus packs unchanged). Campus turns it on.
2. Strain: when Trust < floor while dating or exclusive, she enters visible strain (status text + mood line hook). Trust back ≥ floor clears it. After 2 in-game days of continuous strain, the engine breaks up.
3. Breakup (every path: engine strain, authored, rivalry): apply the scar exactly once per genuine breakup; repeated notifications/replays never reapply. Record it in persistent history (survives recovery, reunion and save/load). Clear the commitment agreement. She stays socially reachable.
4. Repair: eligible when ≥ 2 in-game days since the breakup AND Trust ≥ her bar (sheet `dating.repairTrustBar` override, else world default). Then a reconciliation ask she accepts (engine-validated from state + the interpreted turn) restarts at dating with a fresh agreement, never straight to exclusive.
5. Thresholds only make asks eligible; they never grant consent or intimacy. No extra model call; no real-time timers.
6. Studio: Relationship stages section (floor, days, scar, delay, default bar). Sheet editor: per-heroine repair bar field.

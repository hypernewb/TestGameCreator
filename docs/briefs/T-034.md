# T-034: Jealousy (C1)
Role: implementer · Platform: codex · Milestone: C1

## Goal
If she likes or dates you and sees or hears you being close with another heroine, she reacts: pointed comments, a cooler mood, a confrontation. Affairs stay possible, with consequences.

## Read only
- `docs/map/modules.md` (romance-network, suspicion, dating), `docs/design/stages.md`; then only what you need.

## May change
- suspicion/romance-network modules or new `packages/modules/jealousy.mjs`; prompt injection; packs; contracts; studio section; `tests/jealousy.test.mjs` (new); report.

## Do
1. Triggers: she witnesses a flirt or intimate act with another heroine, or hears about it through the existing suspicion/exposure path.
2. Jealousy meter 0–10 per heroine, raised by triggers and scaled by stage (acquaintance ×0.5 and only if Like ≥ 50, dating ×1, exclusive ×2); decays 1 a day.
3. Effects: at ≥ 3 a cooler-tone hint in the prompt; at ≥ 6 Trust −2 once plus a confrontation hook. Never triggers a breakup directly (the T-012 Trust-floor strain is the only engine breakup path).
4. Flavour by traits (insecure gets louder, guarded gets colder, competitive challenges you).

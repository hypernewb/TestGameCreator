# T-108: Scene voice: lush narrator persona, speech profiles, no more interviewing
Role: prompt-engineer · Platform: codex (planned) · Milestone: CQ-B

## Goal
Conversations read like VU:
- the narration is lush and a little overwritten;
- every character sounds unmistakably like herself;
- she says things and leaves hooks instead of interviewing the player;
- the new conversational and calm modes stay on during firsts;
- the narration may "puppet" the player (owner likes it).

All of it ships as pack options that work on any pack.

## Prerequisite
The uncommitted T-conversation-workflow patch must be reviewed and landed first, with the owner's OK. It touches vendor/venus/scenePrompt.mjs, packages/ai/{service,beats}.mjs, public/{play,vn,turn-presentation,app}.mjs and tests. Build on that patch; never revert it.

## Do
1. **Narrator persona block.** The lush default asks for VU-like texture: sensory detail, body language, and a beat of her inner thoughts. `lean` keeps today's style. The persona is a prompt section chosen by the pack option, not hardcoded strings.
2. **Speech profiles.** Inject a compact profile for each speaking character: 1–2 tics, rhythm and one sample line.
   - Studio warns when two characters share a tic or pet phrase.
   - A lint warns when a reply gives character A's tic to character B.
3. **Stop the question pressure.** Remove "End with a specific question or offer…". Replace it with: "end on something she does, says or wants; a question at most one reply in three". A lint counts question endings (warn only on cloud, per cloud-loose-local-strict).
4. **Let her leave.** Remove the "she never leaves / don't end the scene" style bans. The NPC may exit with a hook (a reason, or a next-time line). The exit becomes a normal scene-end event, so T-112's summary can fire.
5. **Keep the modes on during firsts.** Conversational and calm phases stay active while a first is available. The first is offered as an opportunity in the scene, not a mode switch.
6. **Allow puppeting.** The narration may write the player's reactions and the action they asked for. It still never makes the player's state-changing decisions: accepting, consent, promises, travel or spending.
7. **Every removed "never" ships with a "do this instead" line** (memory rule).

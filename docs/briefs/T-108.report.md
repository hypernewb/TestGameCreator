## Changes
- **Persona (Do 1).** `pack.narratorPersona` (`lush`|`lean`, default `lean`; the social creator template sets `lush`) selects a NARRATOR PERSONA section via `narratorPersona(pack)` in scenePrompt. The old hardcoded "romance novelist" line is gone.

- **Question pressure (Do 3).** "End with a specific question…" removed; replaced by "End on something she does, says or wants; a question at most one reply in three." New soft lint `question-ending` (style check: warn-only on cloud) fires when the last 3 replies have more than one question ending. Required consent check-ins are exempt.
- **Let her leave (Do 4).** Departure bans removed; an NPC may leave with a reason or next-time line by setting `end_scene` (a normal scene end, so T-112 can hook it). INITIATIVE now allows "a reason to leave".
- **Firsts keep modes (Do 5).** `context.first` no longer disables conversational mode or the continuation fast path; the first is framed as an opportunity within the conversation.
- **Puppeting (Do 6).** With `allowPlayerReactions`, player-interior narration is allowed (lint + `dropStock`); new `player-decisions` lint still blocks narrated accept/consent/promise/travel/spend unless the player asked and outcomes committed it.

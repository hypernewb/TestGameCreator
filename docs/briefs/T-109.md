# T-109: Delivery verdict: stats colour what the player actually says and does
Role: implementer · Platform: codex (planned) · Milestone: CQ-B (after T-108)

## Goal
The owner wants stats to show in the writing: "If your charm sucks your flirt sucks; weak fitness, the touch is less effective."

Before the model call, the engine decides how well the player's move lands and sends one line, for example:

> `Your delivery this turn: Charm 2, fumbled. Write the player's actual flirt as a clumsy line, then she reacts to that.`

The same verdict scales what the act earns, so the text and the hearts agree. Stats grow visibly, so early clumsiness turns into competence.

## May change
- **New `packages/modules/delivery.mjs`** (pure). `verdictOf(pack,state,act)` returns `{stat,value,tier,label,line,mult}`.
  - Tiers are `fumbled`, `plain`, `smooth` and `great`.
  - The tier comes from the stat rank plus a deterministic roll seeded by campaign and turn, so it is repeatable.
  - The act→stat map is pack data (`deliveryStats`: flirt→charm, joke→wit, touch/physical→fitness, help-study→focus …), editable on the Studio rules screen.
- **Prompt.** Replace the "only slightly color" PLAYER DELIVERY text with the verdict line. Include one concrete example sentence per tier from the pack's `deliveryExamples`; the social template ships defaults.
- **Pricing.** Apply `mult` in act pricing. The ledger and summary show something like "landed smooth (Charm 9)".
- **Starting stats.** New pack option `startingStats`:
  - `floor`;
  - `spread`: one strength and one weakness (the social template default);
  - `chosen`: the player picks on the creator/new-game screen.
- **Growth.** Verdict events feed the existing `learnFromFailure`. Level-ups surface in T-112's summary; this task only emits the event.
- **Tests:** new `tests/delivery-verdict.test.mjs`.

## Do
1. Classify the player's act (existing scene-acts), map it to a stat, and pick a verdict. If there is no act, or the move isn't performative, send no verdict line: plain conversation is not graded.
2. Send at most one verdict line per turn, for the dominant act. Tell the model to *write the actual line or gesture at that quality*, not to narrate that it was "awkward".
3. Pricing uses the same deterministic verdict. Record both in the turn record for the Chat Lab.
4. Everything is pack-driven and works with a non-campus stat kit, e.g. a fantasy pack with Presence and Grace.

## Acceptance
- Unit tests pass on these points:
  - the same seed gives the same verdict;
  - a higher stat shifts the tier distribution;
  - `mult` is applied;
  - plain talk gets no verdict;
  - a custom stat kit works (T-076).
- A Chat Lab mock run shows the verdict for each turn.
- In an owner-approved live run, a fumbled flirt is written as an actual bad line in at least 3 of 4 fumbled turns (record this in the report).
- `npm test` is green.

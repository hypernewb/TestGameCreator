# T-110: Character goals: her quest that you help with
Role: implementer · Platform: codex (planned) · Milestone: CQ-A

## Goal
Each NPC wants something bigger than this week's wish: a goal with 2–4 steps that the player can help with. In the owner's words, "some goal she wants to achieve that you must try to help them achieve"; quest markers are fine.

She raises the goal in her own voice, progress is tracked, and helping her moves the route forward. Stages can require goal steps.

## May change
- **New `packages/modules/goals.mjs`** (pure; state lives under `goals.<npc>`).
  - Sheet field `characterSheets[].goal`: `{id,title,why,steps:[{id,title,hint,complete:<condition>|'narrative',reward}],secret?}`.
  - Commands:
    - `goals.advance`, validated by the engine through conditions, or as a model-proposed act checked against the step's hint;
    - `goals.reveal`.
- **Prompt.** Add one compact line for the NPC in the scene: her current goal, the next step, and "bring it up yourself when natural". The director may pick a goal step as a scene beat.
- **Conditions.** Add a new `goalStep` condition, so progression stages and firsts can require it.
- **Player UI.** A quest marker in the existing character/relationship panel: one row with the goal title, step n/m and the hint. This is a data hookup only and uses the existing panel styling (Game UI redesign is paused).
- **Studio.** A goal editor in sheet-editor: title, why, a list of steps, and how each step completes (a condition picker or "story decides").
- **Tests:** new `tests/goals.test.mjs` and a browser test for the goal editor.

## Do
1. **Gradual reveal.** A goal stays hidden until she mentions it or Trust reaches a threshold (a pack setting).
2. **Step completion:**
   - Condition-backed steps complete deterministically.
   - `narrative` steps complete only when the model reports a matching act **and** the engine validates it against the step's hint. The model never writes state freely.
3. **Rewards** are typed consequences: stats, flags or unlocks.
4. **Wants vs goals.** Weekly wants stay as they are; goals are the long arc. A want can be tagged as a goal step.

# T-112: Reaction flash + scene-end summary (replacing the ledger chatter)
Role: implementer · Platform: codex (planned) · Milestone: CQ-B

## Goal
The owner says VU's best feedback is a visual flash in the chat when you do something she likes, plus a summary at the end.

Today the player gets a stream of ledger lines: "Selma Aydın took that in stride", "Action completed", stat arrows, a pasted weather paragraph. Replace them with a flash on the line that earned it and one summary card when the scene ends.

## Prerequisite
- T-conversation-workflow must land first: it touches public/{play,vn,turn-presentation}.mjs.
- The Game UI visual redesign is paused. This task is behaviour plus a small component in the existing styling, not a redesign. The owner must confirm that reading before dispatch.

## Do
1. **Flash.** When an act is priced positive or negative, attach a small icon and a short reason to the player's line (or her reply), in the pack's hint style. Reasons can stay vague, per the hidden-reasons preference. Show no numbers for hidden stats.
2. **Remove the noise:**
   - "Action completed.";
   - duplicate "liked that" lines;
   - hidden chemistry and inhibition numbers;
   - the "took that in stride" line when a stage advanced in the same turn;
   - the location paragraph that priceActs re-emits.
3. **Summary card on scene end** (exit, time skip or location change). It shows:
   - her mood;
   - visible stat changes;
   - the stage reached or unlocked;
   - firsts;
   - goal progress (T-110);
   - delivery growth (T-109);
   - the next hook she left.

   One card per scene. It can be dismissed and is also kept in the log.
4. **Celebrations.** A stage-up or a newly opened romance keeps a single celebratory line.

## Acceptance
- Unit tests:
  - the ledger→flash mapping;
  - the summary aggregates a multi-turn scene correctly;
  - the "stride + stage-up" contradiction can't happen;
  - the `ledger` option keeps the old output (back-compat).
- Browser test: a 4-turn mock scene shows flashes on lines and one summary at the exit. No hidden numbers appear in the DOM text.
- `npm test` and `npm run test:browser` pass.

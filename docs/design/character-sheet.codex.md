# Character sheet: design card
Status: draft · Tickets: T-013 · Independent Codex second draft

## Goal (what the player feels)
Her tastes, voice, limits, friendships and changing ambitions make learning about her useful; writers author a whole person and creators can edit both prose and deterministic mechanics. Full depth is the owner direction in [owner answers](../research/owner-answers.md); the proposals below are undecided.

## What we have (self-audit, cite docs/map)
Maps: [contracts](../map/contracts.md), [packs](../map/packs.md), [modules](../map/modules.md), [AI](../map/ai.md). Authoring lives in pack JSON, not a unified heroine contract; [contracts](../../packages/contracts/index.d.ts) types runtime actors only as name/location, plus ActProfile and ArcTrack.
Inventory below covers all actor keys found across packs, campus character-linked records, and optional profile controls read by mapped modules. Runtime scores, discoveries, consent and memories are state, not authored personality.
- Identity: `actors[].id,name,location` identify/place her for all modules; `age,identityTags` support adult/eligibility checks in [intimacy](../../packages/modules/intimacy.mjs)/[dating](../../packages/modules/dating.mjs). `pronouns,description,style,goal,traits,voice,likes,dislikes,backstory` feed [context](../../packages/ai/context.mjs); `traits` also feeds dating trait matches. [Campus](../../packs/campus.json) contains these; `year,major,dorm,appearance` are optional context reads, absent as separate campus actor fields.
- Presentation: campus `portrait,poses[].id,image` are art metadata, not relationship controls; `conversationStarters` are authored entry suggestions; `reactions.skip,ok,good,great` are authored activity responses. `examples` also exists in [illustrated workshop](../../packs/00-illustrated-workshop.json), but is not forwarded by contextFor's actor block. These require presentation/prompt consumers, not new stat rules.
- Routine: campus `schedules[].actor,period,stops[].at,location`, plus [after-hours](../../packs/after-hours.json) `scheduleExceptions[].id,actor,location,startTick,endTick` → [world.schedule](../../packages/modules/social.mjs); calendar/time is world-level.
- Attraction: campus `datingProfiles[].character,initialChemistry,initialPhase,agreements,thresholds.{crush,date,exclusive},preferences[].{id,type,stat,min,flag,value,weight,visibility},signals[].{id,event,match,delta,label,repeatable}` → dating. Optional `initialAgreement,initialSatisfaction,scoreVisibility,satisfactionVisibility,eligibility.{anyTags,allTags,excludedTags,requiredFlags,forbiddenFlags},allowWhileExclusive,agreementCooldownTicks,agreementSatisfactionCost,agreementGates,maintenance`; preference variants add `label,max,dimension,trait,status` ([dating](../../packages/modules/dating.mjs)).
- Dating policy detail: `agreementGates[].{agreement,minSatisfaction,satisfactionCost,minStage,minRank,requiredScene}` and `maintenance.{strainedAt,breakupAt,breakupCooldown,decayPerTick,signals[].{id,event,match,delta,witnessed,agreements}}` → dating; these are deterministic relationship policy, not prose.
- Gifts/pacing: campus `romanceTempoProfiles[].character,advancement,ranks[].{id,name,points,ceiling},gifts.{periodTicks,limitPerPeriod,repeatDecayPercent,preferences[].{item,reaction,points,chemistry}},signals[].{id,event,match,points,cooldownTicks},jealousy.preset` → [romance-tempo](../../packages/modules/romance-tempo.mjs); optional `initialPoints,ranks[].scene,birthday.{day,date,cycleDays,giftMultiplier,forgottenPoints,minPoints,text,forgottenText},neglect.{afterTicks,fuseTicks,minPoints,points,satisfaction,text,warningText,defusedText},jealousy.{points,satisfaction,cooldownTicks,agreements,events}` belong to the same reader (after-hours supplies neglect).
- Act tastes: campus `actProfiles[].character,entries[].{id,kind,target,points,reason,memory,when.private}` → [acts](../../packages/modules/acts.mjs); optional `fallback.{landed,missed},sizeMultiplier.{small,medium,large},sceneCap,repeatDecayPercent` are typed in contracts. Narrative likes/dislikes alone do not price acts.
- Intimacy: campus `intimacyProfiles[].character,openness,casual,consentPolicy,consentDuration,allowedCategories,cooldown,requiresAgreement,requirements.{attraction,phases},interests[].{id,category,label,visibility},aftercarePreferences[].{id,option,reaction,satisfactionDelta,visibility}` → intimacy, except profile `requiresAgreement` is not read by its eligibility function. Optional `blockedCategories,requirements.satisfaction,missedAftercareDelta,stylePreferences[].{id,style,reaction,satisfactionDelta,visibility},disclosure.{honestyDelta,agreementDeltas}` → intimacy; styles appear in after-hours. Categories, privacy locations, content level, sobriety and aftercare options are world data.
- Touch: [touch-ladder](../../packages/modules/touch-ladder.mjs) has shared steps `id,name,minTrust,lust,trustGain,public,sober` and world privacy settings; no per-character body-region sensitivity or touch veto sheet. A scored `touch` act is not a dedicated hard-limit model.
- Arc/payoffs: campus `arcTracks[].character,traits[].{id,initial},drift[].{act,target,trait,delta},stages[].{id,name,when,voice,flag}` → [arc](../../packages/modules/arc.mjs); contracts additionally allow trait `label` and drift `event`. Campus `firsts[].{id,character,rung,title,opening,trigger,checkpoints[].{id,cue,discover},interest,cg}` → [firsts](../../packages/modules/firsts.mjs); `endings[].{id,name,character,priority,requires,text}` → [endings](../../packages/modules/endings.mjs). Scene conditions/flags are world authored; arc voice is stage-specific prompt flavour.
- Social/contact: campus `phoneContacts`, `phoneMessages[].{id,character,text,trigger,replies[].{id,text},offer.{location,expiresIn}}` → [phone](../../packages/modules/phone.mjs); `suspicionProfiles[].{character,confrontAt}` (optional `curiousAt,decayPerTick,deadlineTicks`) → [secrecy](../../packages/modules/secrecy.mjs). `initialNpcRelationships[].{a,b,affinity}` in [npc-social pack](../../packs/npc-social.json) → [npc-social](../../packages/modules/npc-social.mjs). Named biography connections currently exist as prose, not a character link roster.
- Other supported character records: `initialStats`/`statDefinitions` → [stats](../../packages/modules/stats.mjs); `initialBonds[].{character,dimension,value}`/`relationshipDimensions[].{id,name}` → [bonds](../../packages/modules/bonds.mjs); `relationshipStates[].{id,name}`/`relationshipProgressions[].{character,initial,transitions[].{from,to,event,match,requires.{flags,bonds,stats[].{character,stat,min,max}}}}` → [progression](../../packages/modules/progression.mjs); `romanceAgents[].{character,autonomy,pursuitThreshold,commitThreshold,rivalThreshold,leaveThreshold,rivalryVisibility},npcChemistry[].{from,to,value},initialNpcRomances[].{a,b,phase,agreement,satisfactionA,satisfactionB}` → [romance-network](../../packages/modules/romance-network.mjs). These remain linked world/profile records, not duplicates in actor prose.
Gaps: no dedicated voice fingerprint, occupation/home fields, unified birthday identity field, structured behavior contracts, named social roster or unified sheet; no typed pack-level Character definition. Prose, score rules and state can disagree unless the creator shows their connections.

## Owner decisions (dated; every number lives here)

## Creator option (field, modes, campus default; creator UI screen)
Proposed World creator → Characters → Sheet: Identity & life; Personality & voice; Tastes & gifts; Dating & intimacy; Routine; Behavior; Social links; Arc & payoffs; Art & author notes. Basic/advanced views edit the same record; campus opens basic with its current data imported, and full sections remain accessible. Every mechanic remains a per-world option, with optional character overrides; no new content is inferred on import.
Required proposal: stable ID, name, initial location; age/adult eligibility when romance/intimacy is enabled; enabled modules' existing required references/fields. Rich prose, voice fingerprint, birthday, outside-world links, lore and art stay optional. Missing optional prose stays blank; missing enabled-module requirements block publish with a field-level message.
Use chips for traits/tastes, item and actor pickers for references, repeatable rows for links/rules, a weekly grid plus exception list for routine, and stage cards with condition/effect editors for arcs. Label fields “changes mechanics”, “narration only” or “art”; show linked profile values and inherited world policy beside each override.
Proposed authoring representation: one character view keyed by actor ID, serializing to existing actor/profile tables in the thin slice; define a typed unified authoring contract before adding new fields. Keep runtime consent, scores, memories and discoveries out of the reusable sheet. Validate duplicate IDs, missing references, schedule overlap and contradictory enabled limits; imported prose never silently becomes a rule.
Seed-word auto-fill: borrow Venus's [seedWords](../../.venus/prompts/seedWords.ts) inspiration pool and [characterPrompt](../../.venus/prompts/characterPrompt.ts) structured draft approach. Let the writer pick a word and a premise, request selected blank prose sections, then review changes per field; keep filled fields locked unless selected. Actions, limits, consent policy and intimate content require explicit writer choices; generation never saves these as inferred defaults.

## Thin slice → full version
Thin: a joined editor for current campus actor/profiles, preserving all authored fields and world options; add optional prose depth and named links with no new mechanical meaning. Preview both compact narration notes and deterministic rules; round-trip existing packs without loss.
Full: typed touch/kink profiles, behavior event rules, birthday/weather routines and social-link initialization, then arc-aware voice and art. Each mechanic needs module integration, pack migration, creator editing and tests; prompts only narrate committed outcomes. Do not port reference scoring calls: owner requires one interpretation/narration call per turn and engine judgment.
Cheap experiment: author two contrasting sheets for the same neutral encounter, then compare voice, gift outcomes, schedule availability and refusal explanations while verifying unchanged engine outcomes when only P fields differ.

## Fun rubric notes (agency, surprise, payoff, repetition)
Agency: learn tastes and act on real rules; limits and optional mechanics are explicit. Surprise: routines and social links explain unexpected meetings. Payoff: goals and stage voice make progress personal. Repetition: distinct speech examples, discoveries and stage behavior vary narration; cadence remains engine-controlled rather than promised by prose.

# Synthesis of Research Documents

## Decided
- Immediate milestone: connect the core play loop (input, response, consequences, feedback, continuity) into one complete playable encounter; the release criterion is a coherent whole, not individual modules. (system-priorities-2026-10-01.md, lines 7, 11)
- Conversation quality is the first experiential target, delivered with freedom, independent relationship progression and UI reliability; meaningful choices and worthwhile daily goals come after foundational repairs; conversation repair means scene lifetime, character voice, recent context/memory and presentation. (system-priorities-2026-10-01.md, lines 9, 15, 35, 41, 101-105)
- Priority tiers: 1 believable conversations, 2 freedom/input/choices/outcome delivery, 3 relationship depth/firsts/progression, 4 daily gameplay (activities, schedules, money, stats, minigames), 5-8 director/gossip/drama, phone/plans/calendar, creator/imports, art/audio/packaging later or proposed. (system-priorities-2026-10-01.md, lines 90-98)
- Benchmarks: Venus University principal experience, SillyTavern conversation reference, Heartmorrow/RP Suite relationship feedback and aftermath. (system-priorities-2026-10-01.md, line 11)
- First campus playable delivers a meaningful romantic first within 15 minutes; ordinary typed exchange is about 5-6 dialogue boxes; current NPC replies are too short. (loop-comparisons.md, line 5)
- Product: erotic dating sim; a creator outputs Venus-like gameplay across many worlds with modules on top; the audience is a creator for others from day one; the first playable must nail seduction as deduction and erotic payoff/collection. (design-interview.md, lines 6, 12, 25-26; GAME-DESIGN.md, line 48)
- Kept from Venus: seduction as deduction, authored firsts, one focus NPC per free-typing scene, visible hearts, map plus phone hub; Persona 5 social-link ranks as the reward reference. (loop-comparisons.md, line 5; GAME-DESIGN.md, line 60)
- Fun sources: seduction as deduction and erotic payoff/collection first, then juggling time/stats/money and director surprises. (design-interview.md, line 21)
- Player fantasy: sandbox with corruption/change arc, one deep romance, cheating while partnered without getting caught. (design-interview.md, line 15; GAME-DESIGN.md, line 41)
- Run shape is modular per game (romance dating sims, later isekai roguelite, chill forever-sandbox); proof worlds are a Venus-like campus then a setting from a SillyTavern card. (design-interview.md, lines 16, 98; GAME-DESIGN.md, lines 45, 188)
- Scene contract: input primary, echo the player action in 2nd person with no added intent and never verbatim quotes, one focus NPC, replies are 1-3 sentence beats ending on a hook, complete the player's action (refusals only from state), empty or vague input advances, a 3-5 input budget closes the scene, never write player dialogue beyond the echo. (GAME-DESIGN.md, lines 75-84; playtest-transcript.md, line 42)
- Scene feel: the NPC pushes back on touch in character without shutting the scene down and ends on a hook; clicking past a finished reply auto-continues and moves the player; a three-word meta command ("Flirt with them") becomes a concrete physical beat; characterization surfaces as a hook from a trait and Lili the streamer hands the player the next move. (playtest-transcript.md, lines 43, 65, 142, 145)
- NPCs take initiative only after fully reacting to the player; conversation duration emerges from engagement; minor awkwardness fades when the conversation ends. (system-priorities-2026-10-01.md, lines 21, 23, 192)
- Touch in any scene has consequences; prose may stand ahead of state while state does not follow and she remembers and treats the player differently, but the hard rule for intimacy is that state wins, with consent in state and the engine blocking before the model writes. (design-interview.md, lines 39, 87-88; GAME-DESIGN.md, lines 73, 96-97)
- Keep completed actions when reply generation fails; the error dialog offers Retry / Return to main menu with no progress lost; "Change it" on LLM_BLOCKED appends a random-encounter directive. (system-priorities-2026-10-01.md, line 197; playtest-transcript.md, lines 10, 103)
- Milestone rule: the model tags/proposes, the engine clamps/prices/records, replay never calls the model, fun first and optimization later, one model call per normal turn. (NEXT-MILESTONES.md, lines 6-9; GAME-DESIGN.md, lines 10, 88)
- Local evaluation model `qwen/qwen3.8-27b` at `http://localhost:1234/v1`; the owner plays on a cloud model, which is fine because explicit content works there. (system-priorities-2026-10-01.md, line 168; NEXT-MILESTONES.md, line 9; GAME-DESIGN.md, line 36)
- Model writes NPC memories freely into state, plus memories and random events; it tags what happened and how it landed and the engine turns tags into numbers; end-of-scene bookkeeping is a separate LLM pass emitting stat deltas plus one-line reasons with a deterministic gate check against player stat. (design-interview.md, lines 11, 85-86; GAME-DESIGN.md, lines 87-88; playtest-transcript.md, line 81)
- Player view is a journal of discoveries (likes, limits, facts), not raw NPC memory, and uneditable; saying no stays in fiction (explain in fiction, offer alternatives, redirect in story, game-master voice), never a system error. (design-interview.md, line 89; GAME-DESIGN.md, lines 25, 104)
- Non-negotiables: explicit adult characters, consent specific/current/withdrawable and in state, model output is untrusted text where only engine-priced tags/checks/authored effects change numbers or gates (memories exception), deterministic replay. (GAME-DESIGN.md, lines 192-196)
- Three independent relationship values (like, lust, trust) with character-specific priorities; the main lasting consequence of an interaction is an increase in them. (system-priorities-2026-10-01.md, lines 25, 27)
- Progression order: discover and act on her preferences first, then help with problems, then relationship milestones. (system-priorities-2026-10-01.md, line 31)
- Repeated flirting hits an encounter cap with diminishing gains and needs something more meaningful; an encounter ends when she completely loses interest or the player exceeds boundaries. (system-priorities-2026-10-01.md, lines 29, 33)
- Lust has no hard ceiling and eases toward an evolving baseline as game time passes. (system-priorities-2026-10-01.md, lines 185-186)
- Small visual reactions during conversation with a recap afterwards (light flashing hearts, smiles); serious negative outcomes use capped stat losses without a persistent grudge; remember preferences, disclosures, promises, boundaries and shared moments. (system-priorities-2026-10-01.md, lines 187, 191, 193)
- Relationships show visible hearts with hidden reasons as the deduction game, not Venus's star flash. (GAME-DESIGN.md, line 106; design-interview.md, line 111)
- Likes puzzles go deeper: hints not exact answers, open/closed likes list, clues from acts, gossip, recap and discoveries. (GAME-DESIGN.md, line 31; NEXT-MILESTONES.md, line 43)
- Erotic loop: discovery of turn-ons/limits by deduction plus journal, ladder of firsts (authored milestone, gallery unlock), director-made mood/moment, mastery and variety. (design-interview.md, line 72; GAME-DESIGN.md, line 128)
- Hidden traits drift and thresholds reach named stages that visibly change voice, outfits, what she'll say yes to and unlocked scenes; the creator authors each track. (design-interview.md, line 56; GAME-DESIGN.md, line 112)
- What changes over a run: NPCs, the player (reputation, confidence), negotiated relationship agreements, NPC-to-NPC relationships; getting caught yields a free-typing confrontation, breakup/bad ending, a negotiating opening or ripple drama. (design-interview.md, lines 62, 67)
- Routes never close: permanent closers become hurdles with a cooldown/repair thread, an affair stays possible even after a rival wins, and a partner becomes a source of suspicion. (NEXT-MILESTONES.md, line 37; GAME-DESIGN.md, line 35)
- Steal from Venus, top 5 by fun-per-effort: hidden NPC schedules, affection-gated relationship stages, personality/seed-word prompts, player action parsing with stat deltas, crush mechanic. (venus-skill-digest.md, lines 8, 16, 24, 32, 38-42)
- Core hub is map plus phone: the map shows locations, activities and who might be there, the phone gets texts, invites, rumors and director events, free-text is always available, and cards or activities show stat/gold ranges and who might be there. (design-interview.md, lines 29-33; GAME-DESIGN.md, line 56)
- Time is a clock: the UI shows time and costs, activities have hours, scenes keep a 3-5 input budget and can be extended by paying time, and her schedule decides when she leaves. (GAME-DESIGN.md, line 30; NEXT-MILESTONES.md, line 40)
- Time advances in slots (DAY → EVENING) with a full-screen day card and scene title, one scene per slot, and conversation advances time as it unfolds; the morning card carries date, days left, weather, callbacks and rumor. (playtest-transcript.md, line 85; system-priorities-2026-10-01.md, line 207; GAME-DESIGN.md, line 55)
- Broad periods govern availability and exact hours govern activities; check availability and allow character-specific rearrangement; remind without forcing attendance; lateness and missed meetings get character-specific aftermath. (system-priorities-2026-10-01.md, lines 204-208)
- Plans: vague invitations stay tentative, a concrete spoken agreement confirms the plan and auto-saves with a confirmation shown, purchases are billed, invites become calendar events, and calendar repair belongs to the connected flow. (system-priorities-2026-10-01.md, lines 200, 202-203; GAME-DESIGN.md, line 94)
- Typed actions happen (`move`, `buy`, `invite`, `extend`, `wait`) with a `refusal` object replacing fixed error strings; the model may invent places, items and plot threads that persist but not new NPCs or character facts; creativity pays with a bonus for specific input. (GAME-DESIGN.md, lines 16, 20-21; NEXT-MILESTONES.md, lines 39, 41)
- Scene types: Activity (minigame, no AI), encounter/hangout/date (free typing, 3-5 inputs, end card), director event (authored opener, choices or short free-typing middle), intimate (authored first time, free-typed repeats); activities turn into encounters on a chance roll or a met condition. (design-interview.md, lines 34, 39; GAME-DESIGN.md, lines 65, 73)
- Minigames are clue/deduction and timing/reflex only, never recall quizzes or typed pitches; every activity offers one, skipping gives the middle of the range, pushing luck raises gains toward the top of range with a mishap chance that feeds the director; no arcade minigames in social or intimate scenes, where skill is reading cues and free-typing scores intent. (design-interview.md, lines 10, 79-82; GAME-DESIGN.md, line 163)
- Roguelike 3-card UI is a later layer with deckbuilding later still; the planning hub has balance, stat triangle, suggestions, free-text "Something else?", Bunnyboard, Calendar and Log buttons. (design-interview.md, lines 9, 32; GAME-DESIGN.md, line 170; playtest-transcript.md, line 89)
- Venus's biggest gap is the lack of a game director; ours generates events, scenes, challenges and roadblocks and gives the player things to do. (design-interview.md, line 8)
- The director generates four content types (events/invitations, obstacles/rivals, goals/deadlines, temptations) from a template library, the engine picks and fills templates and the AI only narrates, and pacing is fully adaptive (cheating → suspicion, quiet → drama, losing → opportunity). (design-interview.md, lines 43, 48-49; GAME-DESIGN.md, line 137)
- The director becomes game master: stance, moves list in code, a mix of templates and moves, mid-scene complications, NPC agendas, a `dm` line type for asides/explanations/recap, and stakes. (NEXT-MILESTONES.md, line 42; GAME-DESIGN.md, line 154)
- The creator sets the world tone ceiling and a per-world freedom dial for model invention versus authored structure (campus mostly open); structure is a creator option rather than the default, and the freedom model wins where it conflicts with older text. (design-interview.md, line 53; GAME-DESIGN.md, lines 7, 12, 15)
- Creator entry points are SillyTavern cards, a one-paragraph premise and a structured form, reviewed through a guided wizard (premise → cast → places → arcs/firsts → director events) where the AI drafts and the creator edits and approves; first times and arc stages come from a shared library of beat templates with world-specific text drafted at creation. (design-interview.md, lines 92, 96-97; GAME-DESIGN.md, line 175)
- Creator structure settings: freedom dial, firsts mode, per-world bands, open/closed likes, checkpoint cues and choices, agenda seeds, game-master stance. (NEXT-MILESTONES.md, line 45; GAME-DESIGN.md, line 179)

## Conflicts between docs
- **Milestone order:** `design-interview.md` (line 103) says M0 → M3 → M2 → M5; `NEXT-MILESTONES.md` (line 15) says re-planned O2 → C1 → Fun gate → F1. `NEXT-MILESTONES.md` is newer and is linked as the roadmap by `GAME-DESIGN.md` (line 7), so it supersedes.
- **Fiction vs. gates:** `venus-university/playtest-transcript.md` (line 83) reports Venus narrators whose warm reactions disagree with the gate because the LLM never sees gates; `system-priorities-2026-10-01.md` (line 59) wants meaningful progress plus a concise reaction and `GAME-DESIGN.md` (line 91) carries her likes and limits privately so the prose matches the verdict, so ours aims to avoid that divergence.
- **Typing vs. buttons (clarified, not a conflict):** `system-priorities-2026-10-01.md` (line 178) wants no gameplay/action buttons during conversations; `GAME-DESIGN.md` (lines 55, 65, 67) has Activity scenes with minigames or result cards plus a map and phone hub, and `loop-comparisons.md` (line 5) assumes the same hub, so "no buttons" means in-conversation actions only.
- **Visual feedback for likeability:** `GAME-DESIGN.md` (line 107) uses hearts deliberately, not Venus's hidden score with a star flash, unlike `venus-university/findings.md` (line 29).

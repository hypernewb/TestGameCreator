## Method and evidence limits

- **Control**: a Studio or advanced/contract control in `public/studio*.mjs`, `public/creator-*.mjs` or `public/sheet-editor.mjs` writes the key. Checked by regex in `tests/gc62-rel-audit.test.mjs`, which is presence only.
- **Persisted**: the key the control writes is the key the runtime reads in `packages/`. This is key-level, not a save/reopen round trip. Round trips are covered by the cited creator tests where named.
- **Runtime outcome**: a named `test()` in `tests/` checks a runtime result for that rule. Title presence is checked by the audit test; the test bodies were not re-run here beyond the normal suite.
- Row numbers match the test file. Rows the test cannot cite by title are file-level.

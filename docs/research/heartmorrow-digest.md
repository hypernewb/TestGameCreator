# Heartmorrow: ranked steal list (T-019 redo)
Evidence: local checkout, including the existing Skill Seeker [index](../../.heartmorrow/skill/SKILL.md) and [architecture section](../../.heartmorrow/skill/references/architecture/architectural_patterns.json); implementation wins over generated descriptions. Rankings are estimated player fun divided by adoption effort, not measured results. Numbers below describe Heartmorrow unless explicitly labelled proposals.

## Core loop (8 lines)
1. Character/world data, relationship state, recent transcript and selected memories feed streamed dialogue; dialogue alone grants no stats ([conversation service](../../.heartmorrow/apps/server/src/services/conversation-service.ts)).
2. Dates additionally run a structured engagement judge, every turn or every second turn (messages ≥120 characters still trigger it); engagement is −3..3 and drives server-owned rapport ([same service](../../.heartmorrow/apps/server/src/services/conversation-service.ts)).
3. At sitting end a separate structured evaluation proposes deltas, memories and a highlight; server bounds evaluation deltas to ±15 and stats to 0..100 ([constants](../../.heartmorrow/packages/shared/src/constants.ts), [stat service](../../.heartmorrow/apps/server/src/services/stat-service.ts)).
4. Strict JSON parsing plus schema validation rejects malformed proposals; retries/format downgrades can issue further calls ([structured caller](../../.heartmorrow/apps/server/src/llm/structured.ts)).
5. Last-contact and last-in-person-day are separate; rollover applies neglect after 14/10/7/4 days for none/dating/exclusive/cohabiting, at ×1/1/1.5/2 decay ([social rules](../../.heartmorrow/packages/shared/src/social.ts), [clock](../../.heartmorrow/apps/server/src/services/world-clock-service.ts)).
6. Strain checks follow date consequences and day rollover: warning → breakup after 3 days; catastrophic dates bypass warning; only a deliberate date after cooldown can reconcile ([breakup service](../../.heartmorrow/apps/server/src/services/breakup-service.ts)).
7. Spending stamina derives morning/afternoon/evening/night; sleep advances the day and resets stamina; seasons last 28 days, with deterministic holidays/anniversaries ([time rules](../../.heartmorrow/packages/shared/src/time.ts), [clock](../../.heartmorrow/apps/server/src/services/world-clock-service.ts)).
8. Memories rank importance then recency (default 10, at most 2 NPC-life entries); a chronicle buffers highlights and asynchronously folds at 5 lines; transcript summaries trigger every 24 messages ([memory](../../.heartmorrow/apps/server/src/services/memory-service.ts), [chronicle](../../.heartmorrow/apps/server/src/services/chronicle-service.ts), [constants](../../.heartmorrow/packages/shared/src/constants.ts)).

## Top 8, highest fun/effort first
Call policy for every sketch: one existing interpretation/narration call per normal turn; engine computations add zero. No automatic repair request on that path. Source extra calls are flagged individually; proposed adoption adds no runtime background or end-of-scene calls.

1. **A hidden need makes today's encounter different — high fun / low effort.**
   Source: [date dynamics](../../.heartmorrow/packages/shared/src/date-dynamics.ts): six needs (listen, levity, desire, depth, spontaneity, guarded), picked from a stable seed; rapport starts at 50 minus up to 18 for guardedness; positive engagement steps are ×5, negative ×8, with positive damping up to 60%.
   Ours: matches [character-sheet](../design/character-sheet.md) personality and [scene-feedback](../design/scene-feedback.md) engine-picked mood; [flirt-cap](../design/flirt-cap.md) already covers repeated flirting. New part is a temporary need, not another mood score.
   Adopt: extend scenes with an authored eligible-need list; persist one seeded need at scene opening, insert its behavior cue into the existing prompt, and price matching existing interpreted tags in social. Retain the decided five feedback tiers and current consent gates. Rotation is owner Q1.
   Calls: source `judgeTurn` is an **extra per-turn call: reject**; do not import rapport scoring or its judge.

2. **Gift keepsakes with visible diminishing returns — high fun / low–medium effort.**
   Source: [gift service](../../.heartmorrow/apps/server/src/services/gift-service.ts) caps each gift delta at ±6; same-day positive warmth scales 1 → 0.5 → 0, negatives stay full; inventory consumption, count and effects commit together. Optional memory becomes a keepsake. [shop guide](../../.heartmorrow/docs/ADDING_SHOP_ITEMS.md) defines typed effects and transactional funds/stock checks.
   Ours: matches [character-sheet](../design/character-sheet.md) decided liked/disliked gift categories; [flirt-cap](../design/flirt-cap.md) already identifies gift cadence, so this is an extension of existing pacing, not a new gifting system.
   Adopt: extend economy/social gift handling to record item + recipient + applied effect + keepsake event atomically; reuse existing cadence and show its applied reduction through scene-feedback. Propose the source's 1/0.5/0 schedule only as a tuning option (Q2), without stacking a second damping pass.
   Calls: source gift reaction has a dedicated call; typed gifting must use the normal turn's tags and reply, with category effects priced by the engine.

3. **Relationship warnings travel through the phone — high fun / medium effort.**
   Source: [breakup service](../../.heartmorrow/apps/server/src/services/breakup-service.ts) persists `rocks:since`, `beat:pending`, breakup day/count/status; [social rules](../../.heartmorrow/packages/shared/src/social.ts) use dating warmth<22 or tension>72, 3-day warning grace, catastrophic margin 12, scar +5/break capped at 15, and reconciliation after 3 days at warmth≥50. Breakup purges queued warm texts but retains delivered history.
   Ours: **matches card [stages](../design/stages.md)**: warnings, lasting scar, reachable deliberate repair and renewed agreements are already specified; do not re-propose their numbers or infer consent from warmth.
   Adopt: extend dating's committed transitions with an exactly-once pending phone beat keyed by transition ID; invalidate stale queued invitations/affection on breakup; feed current status plus retained history into the next existing narration. Reconciliation remains an explicitly validated repair outcome under stages, not automatic warmth recovery.
   Calls: transition service itself is deterministic; source beat prose uses a separate day-start call. Adopt authored templates queued once per transition, delivered at the next clock boundary, with zero generation calls.

4. **A small texting gain budget, separate contact clocks — high fun / medium effort.**
   Source: [text service](../../.heartmorrow/apps/server/src/services/text-message-service.ts) replies using 12 delivered texts, 5 memories and cross-date chronicle; [social rules](../../.heartmorrow/packages/shared/src/social.ts) cap total positive warmth points at 3 per character/day, with negative effects uncapped by that gain budget. [generation service](../../.heartmorrow/apps/server/src/services/text-generation-service.ts) queues at most one ordinary daily text per eligible character, choosing morning/evening/night; default chance 0.4, forlorn chance 0.25 ([constants](../../.heartmorrow/packages/shared/src/constants.ts)).
   Ours: [day-planner](../design/day-planner.md) already supplies clock, availability and promise outcomes; [modules map](../map/modules.md) supplies social/dating; channel-specific budget/contact clocks are not specified in these cards.
   Adopt: extend social with `lastContactDay` versus `lastMeetingDay` and one phone gain bucket, so texting maintains contact without fabricating an in-person visit. Persist queued/delivered status and scheduled time; use one combined existing interpretation/reply call per player text. Morning queue generation is deterministic from authored eligible beats, once per character/day (Q3).
   Calls: source reply **plus separate text judge violates the limit: reject**; never price from the NPC reply's cheerful tone. Use validated player-action tags and existing capped consequence rules.

5. **Anniversary anchors and a bounded shared history — medium–high fun / medium effort.**
   Source: [social rules](../../.heartmorrow/packages/shared/src/social.ts) stamp `anniv:firstDate/dating/exclusive/cohabiting`; only the current commitment anniversary recurs every 28 days, broken bonds celebrate nothing, reunion re-anchors dating. [chronicle service](../../.heartmorrow/apps/server/src/services/chronicle-service.ts) counts dates separately from gift/milestone lines and folds at 5 highlights; [memory service](../../.heartmorrow/apps/server/src/services/memory-service.ts) reserves space by capping NPC-life memories at 2.
   Ours: matches [day-planner](../design/day-planner.md) future calendar and [stages](../design/stages.md) retained breakup history; [AI map](../map/ai.md) already has memory retrieval/context budgets. Anniversary anchors and cross-channel highlights extend those systems.
   Adopt: stamp anchors from committed dating events; expose upcoming known markers through the same clock/calendar (interval Q4). Append one event-derived highlight at scene close and deterministic gift/transition highlights once per event; include a bounded recent window plus relevant stored memories in the existing context builder, keeping the full ledger.
   Calls: source folding adds background calls whenever buffer≥5 (including gift lines), and summaries every 24 messages add calls: **reject those triggers**. Proposed history rendering/retrieval costs zero; no extra end-of-scene pass.

6. **Confidence wagers in activity deduction — medium fun / medium effort.**
   Source: [two-truths game](../../.heartmorrow/apps/server/src/minigames/two-truths-a-lie.ts): 3 rounds, confidence weights 1/2/3; score=round(100·clamp((correct weighted points−0.5·high-confidence misses)/(3·rounds))). Server-held answer IDs reject duplicate/phantom rounds; the [minigame guide](../../.heartmorrow/docs/ADDING_MINIGAMES.md) bounds rewards to ±10/stat and ≤100 money.
   Ours: [modules map](../map/modules.md) stats/economy/scenes and [day-planner](../design/day-planner.md) existing authored activity durations/effects; synthesis restricts minigames to activities and deduction/reflex, not social/intimate interruptions.
   Adopt: add confidence wagering to an authored activity clue puzzle, keeping 3 rounds as a first playtest value; creator-reviewed clues/answers stay server-side, results dispatch existing activity effects and use existing skip rewards. Do not invent character truths or penalize relationship trust for an arcade miss.
   Calls: source generates rounds at game start; adopt authored packs with seeded ordering, zero runtime calls. Any optional AI drafting runs only when the creator explicitly requests content and reviews it before saving.

7. **Separate texting voice and reciprocal social links — medium fun / low–medium effort.**
   Source: [character schema](../../.heartmorrow/packages/shared/src/schemas/entities.ts) separates `speechStyle`, `textingStyle`, `onlinePersona`, `quirks`, `insecurities`, goals/boundaries and private `creatorNotes`; [social rules](../../.heartmorrow/packages/shared/src/social.ts) represent links as `{targetId,kind}` with mentor↔mentee inverses and other kinds symmetric; [character guide](../../.heartmorrow/docs/ADDING_CHARACTERS.md) offers prompt preview/manual memories.
   Ours: **matches card [character-sheet](../design/character-sheet.md)** all-fields rollout, closed 2–4 traits + free AI notes and Suggest; social relationships already have a [romance-network mapping](../map/modules.md).
   Adopt: map speech/text styles to the existing prompt builder by channel; extend sheet validation to detect missing link targets and display reciprocal mentor/mentee labels; preview exact prompt data before saving. Keep mechanical traits and free prose separate; no new consent/content inference from descriptive fields.
   Calls: authoring Suggest runs only per explicit creator request, never during a normal turn; field validation and channel selection add zero.

8. **Schema checks and diagnostic proposal provenance — indirect fun / low incremental effort.**
   Source: [structured caller](../../.heartmorrow/apps/server/src/llm/structured.ts) strictly parses then `safeParse`s proposals; [stat service](../../.heartmorrow/apps/server/src/services/stat-service.ts) records source, deltas and post-state; [guardrails](../../.heartmorrow/apps/server/src/prompt/guardrails.ts) marks world/character notes as data and forbids invented mechanical outcomes. Evaluation cap is ±15 ([constants](../../.heartmorrow/packages/shared/src/constants.ts)).
   Ours: matches [modules map](../map/modules.md) actions/triggers and [AI map](../map/ai.md) prompt/service separation; deterministic engine ownership is already our contract.
   Adopt: extend AI service diagnostics with rejected proposal reason and link accepted tags to committed event IDs/applied deltas; feed authored data in a clearly delimited block. Reuse each module's existing pricing/caps and actual applied feedback; do not replace them with Heartmorrow's raw stat proposals.
   Calls: source repair retries and output-format downgrade calls **break the normal-turn budget: reject**. Validate the single response locally; use existing failure handling without granting malformed effects. Guardrail prose is no substitute for state gates.

## Anti-list (5)
- Separate rapport, walkout, text and scoring judges: more requests, duplicated grading; use the same validated interpreted turn ([conversation](../../.heartmorrow/apps/server/src/services/conversation-service.ts), [text](../../.heartmorrow/apps/server/src/services/text-message-service.ts)).
- Permanent crisis removal: [crisis rules](../../.heartmorrow/packages/shared/src/crisis.ts) heal 8/day, warn at 40/70, terminal≥90 after 3 crisis days; [crisis service](../../.heartmorrow/apps/server/src/services/crisis-service.ts) memorializes permanently. This conflicts with stages' repairable routes; no crisis-content adoption is recommended.
- Compatibility's universal warmth ceiling: [compatibility service](../../.heartmorrow/apps/server/src/services/compatibility-service.ts) and [stat service](../../.heartmorrow/apps/server/src/services/stat-service.ts) cap incompatible growth below getting-close (warmth 44) even though warmth averages affection/trust/chemistry/comfort/respect. Preserve independent friendship/romance values and authored current boundaries; do not import binary orientation assumptions or make an owner content decision here.
- A second stamina clock: [time rules](../../.heartmorrow/packages/shared/src/time.ts) derive phases from spent stamina; our day-planner requires the existing clock and avoids competing charges. Borrow calendar cues, not this resource model.
- Unreviewed generated truths/client-reported accuracy: [two-truths](../../.heartmorrow/apps/server/src/minigames/two-truths-a-lie.ts) validates shape, not factual grounding; [timing meter](../../.heartmorrow/apps/server/src/minigames/timing-meter.ts) averages submitted accuracy. Use reviewed facts and server-verifiable answers/metrics; server-owned rewards alone do not verify performance.
